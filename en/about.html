About M3U8 Online Player

Project Introduction

M3U8 Online Player is a simple and practical HLS/M3U8 streaming media playback and download tool. It supports online playback, decryption, editing, batch episode playback, history, favorites, and more. Suitable for testing, previewing, learning, and daily use.

Main Features

  • Supports playing videos online by entering M3U8 URLs, compatible with various encryption methods
  • Supports pasting M3U8 content or batch episode lists for playback
  • Supports local file selection for playback
  • Supports switching between players such as ArtPlayer, DPlayer, etc.
  • Supports history and favorites management for quick access
  • Supports one-click download, editing, rotation, and other practical operations
  • Supports embedding the online player into other websites (iframe)
  • Provides download and usage instructions for the M3U8-DL client and command-line tool

Application Scenarios

  • Online playback and testing of M3U8/HLS video streams
  • Batch episode playback and management
  • Decryption and playback of encrypted video streams
  • Video download, format conversion, and local saving
  • Developer debugging and learning streaming protocols
